01The cut is the explanation

Most explanations of how something works are boxes and arrows. A drafting office never drew boxes. It drew the thing itself, cut open along the plane where the mechanism lives, with every part named, measured and made of something.

Blueprint asks Claude to do the same. Pick the real object whose mechanism is the idea, then pick the one cut that shows it: the bore, the seat, the spring, the passage. Then make it move, from a model honest enough that the readout, the dial, the chart and the drawing can never disagree.

Abstract ideas become mechanisms first. A rate limiter is a cistern with a fixed refill. Debounce is a door closer. A circuit breaker is, well, a circuit breaker.

03What Claude does

The skill is a workflow, a drafting kit and a checker. Claude works in this order, and writes the model and the parts list before it draws a single line.

  1. 01
    Size it. A Sheet is one assembly, 15–30 parts, 3–5 views. A Set is several sheets. Sheets are built without asking; a Set is proposed first.
  2. 02
    Truth first. A small model with real dimensions and real physics, pure functions, no DOM. Everything on the sheet reads it.
  3. 03
    Choose the object and the cut. One sentence: what moves, what that motion means, and which cutting plane shows it.
  4. 04
    Parts list. Every part numbered, with its standard size, a real material grade and a quantity.
  5. 05
    Plan the sheet. Main view at a standard scale, details where the mechanism is small, a view in a named direction, a diagram. Laid out in sheet units first.
  6. 06
    Geometry once. Each part is a function that draws into any view, in millimetres of the real object, so a detail is the same code at a larger scale.
  7. 07
    Draw by the rules. Thick visible edges, thin everything else, hatching by material, shafts and balls left unhatched, thin walls blacked in.
  8. 08
    Annotate. Dimensions measured from the geometry, a balloon on every part, words only where a drafter would put them.
  9. 09
    One accent. Exactly one thing carries colour: the thing to watch, usually the working fluid, its density following pressure.
  10. 10
    Make it live. Critically damped motion, an idle tour, drag and keys, live dimensions, a readout in the title block, stillness under reduced motion.
  11. 11
    Check it like a checker. A headless browser plays the tour to a dozen moments and fails on any collision; then Claude reads 4× close-ups of every junction.

04Anatomy of a sheet

These are the rules a drafting checker would mark you down for, from ISO 128, 129, 5455 and 7200. The kit makes them easy; the check makes them hard to break.

One cut

The plane goes through the mechanism. If the reader can't see it, it isn't explained.

Two weights

Visible edges thick, everything else thin, in a ratio of 2:1. Hidden, centre and phantom lines by ISO 128.

Hatch by material

Steel, cast iron, aluminium, brass, rubber, plastic each have a pattern; touching parts never share an angle.

Some things aren't cut

Shafts, pins, bolts, nuts, balls and ribs are drawn whole, as a drafter would.

Measured, not typed

Every dimension is computed from the points it spans. A live one re-measures every frame.

Balloons on parts

A dot on the part, a leader, a number, and a parts-list row. The check fails if a dot misses its part.

One accent

Colour only for the subject: the air, the oil, the steam. The chart's curve is the same colour.

True numbers

The readout, the dial, the curve and the drawing all read one model, so they can't disagree.

BP-003Control / steam28 parts · 6 views

Porter governor

Porter flyball governor with bevel drive, bell crank and double-beat steam throttle valve, in axial section

Negative-feedback proportional control: the speed sets the opening, so more load is carried at a lower speed (droop)

Two iron balls on equal arms lift a weighted sleeve as the spindle speeds up, and the sleeve shuts a balanced double-beat valve through a bell crank. When the load steps up the engine slows, the balls drop, the valve opens, and the engine settles at a new, slightly lower speed. That offset is the droop of a proportional controller, plotted against the governor's own characteristic.

Lever escapement

Swiss lever watch escapement in plan, with a section through the balance staff and its jewel bearings

Turning stored energy into equal ticks: a clock-driven scheduler, a rate-limited event loop

A balance and hairspring swing at 4 Hz whatever the mainspring does; the escapement only lets the train move when the balance says so. Each beat the fork unlocks a stone, the club tooth pushes the stone and the fork pushes the impulse jewel, then the tooth drops onto the other stone and locks, so the escape wheel advances one tooth every two beats. The diagram marks every impulse on the balance's swing.

Pin tumbler cylinder

Authentication by matching a secret: every factor must match (AND)

Each key pin is cut for one depth of the key, so the right key lifts every crown exactly to the shear line between plug and body. The key is cut in 0.5 mm steps; a key one cut wrong leaves pin 3 a millimetre low, its driver straddles the line and the plug will not turn. Only when all five gaps sit on the line does the plug, and its cam, turn.

Circuit breaker

B16 miniature circuit breaker (EN 60898-1) cut on its centre plane

The circuit breaker pattern: closed, open, half-open

A bimetal heated by its own I²R bends until it knocks the latch out, so an overload trips after seconds along an inverse-time curve, while above 4 × In the coil's plunger strikes at once. Open differential

Rear-axle open bevel-gear differential in plan section, with the car in a turn

Splitting one input between two outputs that may run at different speeds: load balancing

The crown wheel turns the carrier, and the carrier always turns at the mean of the two wheels. The spider gears stay still on their pin when the car runs straight and spin only when the wheels differ, in a turn or with one wheel on ice. Because a spider pushes equally on both side gears, the torque splits equally, so the wheel on ice sets what both wheels get.

BP-016Hydraulics15 parts · 7 views

External gear pump

Group 2 external gear pump, 8 cm³/rev, cut across and along its shafts

Positive displacement: a conveyor of fixed-size batches

Two involute gears carry oil round the outside of the bore in sealed tooth spaces, 24 batches of 0.33 cm³ per revolution, and the mesh squeezes them out at the outlet. Flow is displacement × speed less a laminar leak that grows with pressure, so the volumetric efficiency falls as pressure rises, and falls further at low speed. Each space's pressure builds over the last two pitches before the outlet.

Raising the boiling point with pressure; a safety interlock that cannot open while the resource is in use

Water boils where its vapour pressure meets the pressure above it, so a 62 g weight on a Ø3 bore holds 0.86 bar and the water boils at 118 °C instead of 100. Hydraulic disc brake

Bicycle hydraulic disc brake: lever master cylinder and two-piston post-mount caliper in section

Hydraulic force transmission; a master–replica pair where one piston writes and the other follows

Pascal's principle between two pistons. The lever's primary cup first has to pass the timing port before any pressure can build, then the oil pushes the caliper pistons across the pad gap, and only once the pads touch the rotor does the clamp force climb steeply. On release the square seals spring back and pull the pistons off the rotor by exactly the rollback they took.

06Where it's useful

A live section is useful wherever someone needs to see inside something and trust what they see.

Teaching physics and thermodynamics

A model you can push. Students change one thing and watch the pressure, the curve and the numbers follow, all from the same equations.

Floor pump, Bottle jack, Stirling engine, Four-stroke engine, Pressure cooker, Carburettor

Explaining software ideas

Rate limits, debounce, circuit breakers and back-pressure were mechanisms long before they were code. Draw the machine and the abstraction becomes obvious.

Cistern token bucket, Door closer, Circuit breaker, Relief valve, Pin tumbler cylinder, Open differential, Shock absorber, Click pen, Porter governor

Product pages and documentation

Show what is inside the product: the seal, the valve, the spring. A live section sells engineering better than a render.

Hydraulic disc brake, Solenoid valve, Click pen, Floor pump, Pressure cooker

Technician and apprentice training

Real part numbers, real materials, real tolerances. A parts list and balloons that match a teardown.

Four-stroke engine, Wax thermostat, Hydraulic disc brake, Centrifugal pump, External gear pump, Relief valve, Open differential

Control systems and feedback

Setpoints, droop, hysteresis and settling, drawn as the governors and valves that first did it.

Process and hydraulic engineering

Duty points, pump curves, displacement and leakage, at the operating point you choose.

08In your code

A sheet is a single HTML file: inline SVG, inlined CSS and a few kilobytes of plain JavaScript. No framework, no build step at runtime. Embed one anywhere:

<iframe src="floor-pump.html" style="width:100%;aspect-ratio:1200/900;border:0"></iframe>

The drafting kit is pure functions that return SVG strings, so it runs in Node and in the page. Install the package and use it directly:

npm install @harifed/blueprint

import * as K from "@harifed/blueprint/kit/draft";

const V = K.view({ x: 190, y: 780, scale: 1 / 2.5 });
const barrel = K.revolve([[16, 34], [17.5, 34], [17.5, 384], [16, 384]]);
svg += K.section(V, barrel, { mat: "alu", angle: 45 });
svg += K.dim(V, [-17.5, 384], [17.5, 384], { dir: "h", off: -24, prefix: "Ø" });

Every sheet understands the same URL parameters:

09Install

One command copies the skill into ~/.claude/skills/blueprint:

npx @harifed/blueprint

Add --project to install into this project's .claude/skills instead, or --no-examples for a smaller install. Or clone it:

git clone https://github.com/hariFED/blueprint ~/.claude/skills/blueprint

It needs Node.js 20 or later, and Chrome, Chromium or Edge for the checks. Restart Claude Code, then try:

/blueprint explain how a mechanical watch keeps time

/blueprint a section through a bicycle disc brake caliper

10Limits

It takes time. A good sheet is about an hour of Claude's work, most of it in the second look: reading close-ups and fixing what a checker would mark down. Ask for a Set only when one sheet isn't enough.

Explanatory, not manufacturing. Proportions, standard parts and materials are real and the physics is a genuine model, but nobody should machine a part from these sheets.

Flat drawings. Blueprint draws orthographic views and sections, deliberately. If you want a 3D object you can turn, a different skill will serve you better.

The first sheet is a draft. Look at it, say what's wrong, and ask for another pass. The checker catches collisions; only you know whether the explanation lands.

11Questions

Do I need a framework?

No. A sheet is one standalone HTML file with inline SVG and a few kilobytes of plain JavaScript. Open it from disk, host it anywhere, or drop it in an iframe.

Can I edit a sheet afterwards?

Yes. The geometry, the model and the page are separate small files. Ask Claude to change a part, a dimension or the tour, then rebuild with node build.mjs.

Is it accessible?

Each sheet has keyboard controls, a polite screen-reader readout of the live numbers, honours reduced motion by showing a still settled frame, and pauses when off screen.

How accurate are the drawings?

The proportions and standard parts are real and the physics is a genuine model, but these are explanatory drawings, not manufacturing drawings. Treat tolerances and materials as plausible, not certified.

Which themes are there?

Blueprint, paper and dark, all from CSS tokens. Add ?theme=paper to any sheet's URL.

How do I get the best results?

Name the real object if you know it, say what you want to watch, and ask for one Sheet first. Then ask for a second look: Claude finds most problems in the close-ups.
